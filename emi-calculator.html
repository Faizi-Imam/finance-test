Understanding Loan EMIs

An Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are applied to both interest and principal each month, so that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

The EMI Calculation Formula

Loan EMIs are computed using a mathematical compounding formula. The formula is:

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where:

  • P is the Principal loan amount (the initial sum borrowed).
  • r is the monthly interest rate (annual interest rate divided by 12, then divided by 100).
  • n is the loan tenure expressed in number of months (years × 12).

Step-by-Step Example Calculation

Suppose you borrow a Home Loan of ₹20,00,000 (P) at an annual interest rate of 8.5% for a tenure of 20 years (240 months).

  1. Calculate monthly interest rate (r): 8.5 / 12 / 100 = 0.007083 per month.
  2. Calculate total number of instalments (n): 20 × 12 = 240 months.
  3. Evaluate formula components:
    (1 + r)^n = (1.007083)^240 ≈ 5.4385
  4. Plug into the EMI equation:
    EMI = [2,000,000 × 0.007083 × 5.4385] / [5.4385 - 1]
    EMI = [77,030.7] / [4.4385] ≈ ₹17,356 per month
  5. Calculate total payments:
    Total Repayment = 17,356 × 240 ≈ ₹41,65,440.
    Total Interest Payable = ₹41,65,440 - ₹20,00,000 = ₹21,65,440.

As you can see, over a long-term loan tenure like 20 years, the total interest paid (₹21.65 Lakhs) can exceed the original principal borrowed (₹20 Lakhs). This is why choosing a lower interest rate or keeping the tenure shorter significantly reduces your financial burden.

Factors That Affect Your Monthly Loan EMI

  • Principal Sum: Borrowing more money directly leads to a higher EMI. Keep down payments high to reduce your principal.
  • Interest Rate: Higher rates increase the monthly interest component. Shop around for low interest rates or improve your credit rating (CIBIL score) to secure cheaper rates.
  • Tenure: A longer tenure spreads payments out and lowers your monthly EMI. However, it increases the compounding period, resulting in much higher total interest paid.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my EMI amount during my loan tenure?

Yes. If you make prepayments towards your principal balance, banks will usually allow you to choose between reducing your monthly EMI or keeping the EMI same and shortening your overall tenure. Opting to shorten the tenure saves you more interest money.

How does the amortization schedule change in early vs late years?

During the first few years of a loan, most of your monthly EMI goes towards paying off the interest. Over time, as the outstanding principal balance decreases, the interest share of the EMI drops, and a larger portion goes toward repaying the principal.

What is a prepayment fee and does it apply to all loans?

In India, the RBI prohibits banks from charging prepayment penalties on floating-rate home loans. However, fixed-rate loans or personal/car loans may incur prepayment fees ranging from 1% to 4% if you pay off the loan early.
