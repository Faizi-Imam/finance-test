Frequently Asked Questions

Common finance questions answered simply.

How is Home Loan EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n – 1). P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), n = total months (years × 12). A ₹40L loan at 8.5% for 20 years gives ~₹34,700/month EMI.

What is SIP and how does compounding help?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) lets you invest a fixed monthly amount in mutual funds. Thanks to compounding, even ₹5,000/month at 12% annual returns grows to ~₹11.6 lakhs in 10 years — of which only ₹6 lakhs is your investment and ₹5.6 lakhs is wealth gained.

What is the current PPF interest rate?

The PPF interest rate is 7.1% p.a. (as of FY 2025-26), set by the Government of India and reviewed quarterly. PPF has a 15-year lock-in, ₹1.5 lakh/year maximum, and enjoys EEE tax status (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt).

Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime — which is better?

The New Regime offers lower slab rates (5%-30%) but no deductions (except ₹75K standard). The Old Regime allows deductions under 80C (₹1.5L), 80D, HRA, etc. If your total deductions exceed ~₹3.75L, the Old Regime may save more. Use our calculator to compare.

How is GST calculated?

GST Exclusive: Add GST to the original price. ₹10,000 + 18% GST = ₹11,800. GST Inclusive: Extract GST from the total. ₹11,800 inclusive of 18% GST means original = ₹10,000, GST = ₹1,800. CGST and SGST are each half the GST amount for intra-state transactions.

Is the currency converter accurate?

Our currency converter provides indicative mid-market rates for reference only. Actual exchange rates from banks and forex services may differ due to margins, fees, and real-time market fluctuations. Always confirm with your bank before transacting.
