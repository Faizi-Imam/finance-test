Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to Faidence Finance ("we", "our", "us"). This policy explains how we handle information on faidence.com.

1. Data We Collect

We do NOT store any of your financial data. All calculator inputs and results are processed entirely in your browser. No financial data is sent to our servers or any third party.

We may collect:

  • Usage analytics: Pages visited, browser type, device type, approximate location (via analytics).
  • Cookies: Used for analytics and serving advertisements via Google AdSense.

2. How We Use Information

  • To improve our calculators and user experience.
  • To serve relevant advertisements via Google AdSense.
  • To understand traffic patterns and popular tools.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies and web beacons to personalise ads based on your browsing. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense – advertising
  • Cloudflare – CDN, DNS, and security
  • Google Fonts – typography

We do NOT share or sell your personal data.

5. Cookies

Our site uses first-party and third-party cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect ad display but will not affect calculator functionality.

6. No Financial Advice

Faidence Finance provides calculators for educational purposes only. We are not a SEBI-registered advisor, financial institution, or tax consultancy. Always consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

7. Your Rights

Since we don't store personal financial data, there is no user data to access or delete. For questions about analytics or cookies, contact us at support@faidence.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be posted with an updated date.
