About Faidence Finance

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to Faidence Finance. We are dedicated to providing the most accurate, user-friendly, and privacy-first financial calculation tools designed specifically for users in India.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to democratize financial literacy and calculation tools. We believe that everyone should have access to clear, mathematical evaluations of their loans, investments, taxes, and savings without having to pay fee premiums, sign up for spam lists, or compromise their personal financial data.

Why Choose Faidence Finance?

  • 100% Free: No paywalls, no trial periods, and no subscription packages. All our tools are completely free to use.
  • Privacy First (0 Data Stored): Unlike other financial utilities that capture your phone number, email, or salary inputs to sell them as sales leads, we store zero data. All calculations happen instantly inside your browser via local JavaScript.
  • Updated for FY 2025-26: Indian tax regulations and interest rates shift constantly. We regularly review and update our PPF rate configs, tax regime slab calculators, and currency conversion indicators to align with current laws.
  • Premium Experience: We focus on speed, elegant design, and responsive charts so you get a pleasant user experience on both desktop and mobile screens.

Our Values

We believe in absolute mathematical transparency. Financial decisions like buying a home, planning a retirement portfolio, or choosing between income tax regimes are life-altering. By providing formulas, step-by-step example runs, and detailed FAQs alongside our tools, we aim to help you understand the how and why of compound interest, loan amortizations, and tax liabilities.

Monetization & Transparency

To keep our servers running and maintain these tools, we display advertisements via Google AdSense. We do not engage in affiliate marketing or cross-selling of predatory financial products. If you find our tools useful, please whitelist us on your ad-blocker to support free utilities.

Support and Contacts

Faidence Finance is a part of the larger Faidence ecosystem, which includes expert tutoring and educational resources. If you have any suggestions, feedback, or spot a bug in our formulas, feel free to reach out to us at hello@faidence.com or visit our main site at faidence.com.
