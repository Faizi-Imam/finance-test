Frequently Asked Questions

Common finance questions answered simply.

How is Home Loan EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the principal loan amount (P), the monthly interest rate (r), and the tenure in months (n) with the compound formula: [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]. For example, a ₹40L loan at 8.5% for 20 years yields an EMI of approximately ₹34,700/month.

What is SIP and how does compounding help?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) allows you to invest a fixed monthly sum in mutual funds instead of a lumpsum. Over time, earnings from your units are reinvested. Compounding ensures even ₹5,000/month at a 12% annual return grows to ₹11.6 Lakhs in 10 years, where ₹5.6 Lakhs is pure interest profit.

What is the current PPF interest rate?

The PPF interest rate is 7.1% p.a. (as of FY 2025-26), set by the Government of India and reviewed quarterly. PPF has a 15-year lock-in, ₹1.5 Lakhs annual investment limit, and enjoys EEE tax status (exempt from investment, accumulation, and withdrawal taxes).

Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime — which is better?

The New Regime offers lower tax rates (5% to 30%) but disqualifies deductions. The Old Regime offers higher rates but allows HRA, Sec 80C, 80D, and home loan interest deductions. Salaried individuals generally benefit more from the New Regime unless their tax-saving deductions exceed ₹3.75 Lakhs.

How is GST calculated in India?

GST Exclusive adds tax to the base price: ₹10,000 + 18% GST = ₹11,800 total. GST Inclusive extracts tax from the total price: ₹11,800 inclusive of 18% GST means the base price is ₹10,000 and the GST amount is ₹1,800. Intra-state trades split the GST equally into CGST and SGST.

Is the currency converter accurate?

Our currency converter provides indicative mid-market exchange rates for reference. When executing actual forex bank transfers or card payments, providers apply markups and transaction margins. Always verify final transaction rates with your bank.
