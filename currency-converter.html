How Currency Conversion Works

A **Currency Converter** calculates the equivalent value of one currency in another based on their respective exchange rates. The exchange rate is the price of one nation's currency in terms of another currency.

The Mid-Market Exchange Rate

The rate shown in this converter is the **mid-market exchange rate** (also known as the interbank rate). This is the midpoint between the buy and sell rates on global currency markets.

  • Bid Rate: The maximum price that a buyer is willing to pay for a currency.
  • Ask Rate: The minimum price that a seller is willing to accept for a currency.
  • The mid-market rate is highly transparent, but it is **not** the rate you get when exchanging money at a bank, airport booth, or card processor.

Understanding Transaction Markups & Spreads

When you exchange cash or make international payments, service providers add a markup to the interbank rate to cover operational costs and make a profit.

  1. Bank Margins: Traditional banks charge a currency markup ranging from 2% to 5% above the mid-market rate.
  2. Credit Card Markups: Major credit card networks (Visa, Mastercard) charge a foreign transaction fee or currency markup of 1% to 3.5%.
  3. Specialist Services: Modern fintech transfer platforms offer rates closer to the mid-market with transparent flat fees.

Step-by-Step Example Calculation

Suppose you want to convert $1,000 USD to INR. The nominal exchange rate is 1 USD = ₹85.50.

  • Interbank Conversion: 1,000 USD × 85.50 = ₹85,500.
  • Bank Conversion (with 3.5% markup):
    - Effective rate: 85.50 × (1 - 0.035) = ₹82.5075 per USD.
    - Final payout: 1,000 USD × 82.5075 = ₹82,507.50.
    - Total markup fee paid = ₹85,500 - ₹82,507.50 = ₹2,992.50.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do currency exchange rates fluctuate constantly?

Exchange rates are floating and determined by demand and supply in international foreign exchange (forex) markets. Key drivers include interest rate changes by central banks (like the US Fed or RBI), inflation rates, trade balance sheets, economic growth, and geopolitical stability.

Is this converter updated in real-time?

Our converter uses indicative rates updated periodically. It is suitable for general planning, estimates, and educational purposes. Always verify rates with your specific bank or wire service before executing financial transactions.

What is a dynamic currency conversion (DCC)?

DCC is a service offered by overseas merchants or ATMs, letting you pay in your home currency rather than the local currency. While convenient, DCC usually incurs highly unfavorable exchange rates and hidden markup fees. It is almost always better to choose to pay in the **local currency**.
